## Recitals

Aretia operates a public treasury, funded by fees collected on trading of the ACT token (Solana address `[MINT ADDRESS]`), which it disburses to support climate-related projects. Partner operates `[one-sentence description of the project]`. The Parties wish to set the terms on which Aretia provides funding to Partner in support of that project.

⚠️ **Nothing in this Agreement creates an investment, security, partnership, joint venture, or profit-sharing relationship between the Parties.** Aretia's disbursement is a grant or credit purchase as specified in the applicable Exhibit, not a loan, equity investment, or purchase of any interest in Partner. Partner has no claim on ACT treasury assets beyond the specific disbursement(s) described in this Agreement.

## 1. Definitions

- **"Disbursement"** — a transfer of funds or on-chain assets from the Treasury Wallet to Partner under this Agreement.
- **"Treasury Wallet"** — the Solana multisig address `[SQUADS MULTISIG ADDRESS]`, controlled by Aretia.
- **"Funding Type"** — either a **Grant** (Exhibit A) or a **Retirement Purchase** (Exhibit B), as specified for each Disbursement.
- **"Reporting Period"** — the interval specified in the applicable Exhibit during which Partner must submit impact reporting.
- **"Impact Report"** — documentation of how Disbursed funds were used and what outcome resulted, per the metrics specified in the applicable Exhibit.

## 2. Funding

2.1 Aretia will provide Partner with the Disbursement(s) described in **Exhibit A** and/or **Exhibit B**, subject to the conditions in this Agreement.

2.2 Disbursements are made in `[USDT / SOL / ACT — specify]` to the wallet address Partner provides in Section 9 (Notices), unless the applicable Exhibit specifies otherwise.

2.3 Aretia has no obligation to make any Disbursement not yet paid as of the date Aretia terminates this Agreement under Section 7.

2.4 ⚠️ Disbursements depend on ongoing treasury inflows from ACT trading activity, which Aretia does not guarantee. This Agreement is not a promise of a fixed total amount unless a fixed amount is stated in an Exhibit.

## 3. Use of Funds

3.1 Partner will use each Disbursement solely for the purpose(s) described in the applicable Exhibit.

3.2 Partner will not use any Disbursement for `[excluded uses — e.g., general administrative overhead beyond X%, lobbying, political activity]`.

3.3 If Partner cannot use a Disbursement for its stated purpose, Partner will notify Aretia within `[30]` days and the Parties will agree on either a revised use or return of the unused funds.

## 4. Reporting & Verification

4.1 Partner will submit an Impact Report to Aretia at the end of each Reporting Period, containing the information specified in the applicable Exhibit (e.g., photos, third-party registry certificates, metered generation data, GPS-tagged site records).

4.2 ⚠️ For Retirement Purchases (Exhibit B), Partner will provide documentation sufficient for Aretia to verify the credits were issued by `[registry name, e.g. Verra]`, are additional and not double-counted, and were retired (not merely purchased) on Partner's behalf or transferred to Aretia for retirement.

4.3 Aretia may, at its own expense, request a site visit or independent third-party verification of any Impact Report, with reasonable advance notice.

## 5. Public Disclosure

5.1 Partner consents to Aretia publishing the following on its public transparency dashboard and in its own reporting: Partner's name, the Disbursement amount(s), date(s), and a summary of the funded activity.

5.2 Aretia will not publish `[categories of information Partner considers sensitive — e.g., exact site coordinates, beneficiary personal data]` without Partner's prior written consent.

5.3 Partner may reference this Agreement and its relationship with Aretia in its own public communications, subject to `[Aretia's prior review / no restriction — decide]`.

## 6. Representations & Warranties

Each Party represents that:

(a) it has full legal authority to enter into this Agreement;

(b) it is not subject to sanctions, embargoes, or watchlists under `[applicable sanctions regimes, e.g. OFAC]`;

(c) (Partner only) the project described in the applicable Exhibit is real, lawfully operated, and the information provided to Aretia for vetting purposes was accurate in all material respects;

(d) (Partner only) Partner will comply with all applicable anti-money-laundering, anti-corruption, and environmental-registry rules relevant to its operations.

## 7. Term, Termination & Clawback

7.1 This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues until all Disbursements under active Exhibits are complete, unless terminated earlier.

7.2 Either Party may terminate for the other's uncured material breach, on `[30]` days' written notice, if the breach is not cured within that period.

7.3 ⚠️ **Clawback.** If Partner is found to have materially misrepresented its use of a Disbursement, or funds are used for a purpose excluded under Section 3.2, Aretia may require Partner to return the unused or misused portion, and may withhold future Disbursements under any Exhibit.

## 8. No Investment; No Token Rights

8.1 This Agreement does not grant Partner any ACT tokens, governance rights, or equity in Aretia.

8.2 If Partner separately wishes to hold or trade ACT tokens, that is entirely outside the scope of this Agreement and subject to the risks of any open-market token.
