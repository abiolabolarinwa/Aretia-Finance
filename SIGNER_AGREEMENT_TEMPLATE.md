**TREASURY MULTISIG SIGNER AGREEMENT**

This Agreement is entered into as of `[DATE]` between:

**Aretia Finance LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company ("**Aretia**"), and

**`[SIGNER LEGAL NAME]`** ("**Signer**"),

together the "Parties."

## 1. Role

Signer is one of `[N]` members of a Squads multisig (the "**Treasury Multisig**") requiring **`[X]`-of-`[N]`** approval to execute any transaction. Signer's role is to independently review and approve or refuse proposed Treasury Multisig transactions.

## 2. Responsibilities

Signer will, for each proposed transaction:

(a) review the amount, destination, and stated purpose before signing;

(b) check the transaction against the context available (e.g., whether a claimed milestone has independent verification recorded, whether the amount matches what was approved);

(c) decline to sign — and raise the concern to the other signers — if anything is inconsistent, unverified, or unclear;

(d) never sign a transaction based solely on a request from Aretia's founders or team without independently reviewing it.

⚠️ **Signer is not a rubber stamp.** The purpose of this role is to provide a real, independent check on treasury outflows. An approval given without genuine review defeats that purpose and may breach this Agreement.

## 3. Availability

Signer will respond to proposed transactions within **`[X business days — e.g., 5]`** under normal circumstances. If Signer expects to be unreachable for an extended period, Signer will notify the other signers and Aretia in advance. Extended unresponsiveness without notice may be grounds for removal under Section 8.

## 4. Security requirements

(a) Signer will use a dedicated wallet for this role, generated and controlled solely by Signer — never a key provided by Aretia or any other party.

(b) A hardware wallet is required once the Treasury Multisig moves to mainnet with real funds; a software wallet is acceptable for devnet testing only.

(c) Signer will never share their private key or seed phrase with anyone, including Aretia, for any reason.

(d) Signer will independently verify transaction details in their own wallet interface before signing, not rely solely on any third-party UI.

## 6. Indemnification ⚠️

*(Leave for counsel to draft — do not reuse generic boilerplate here.)* Aretia intends to indemnify Signer for good-faith actions taken within the scope of this role, subject to terms counsel will define. Signer should not rely on this section until it has been properly drafted and reviewed.

## 7. Conflicts of interest

Signer will disclose and recuse from reviewing or signing any transaction in which Signer has a personal, financial, or other material interest.

## 8. Term, removal, and replacement

(a) This Agreement continues until terminated by either Party on `[30]` days' written notice, or immediately for a breach of Sections 2 or 4.

(b) On termination, Signer will cooperate in a timely transition of multisig membership, including removal of Signer's key from the Treasury Multisig and, where applicable, addition of a replacement signer.

(c) Aretia will not remove a signer solely for declining to approve a transaction in good-faith exercise of the review described in Section 2.

## 9. Confidentiality

Signer will keep any non-public information encountered in this role confidential, except that this role's *existence* and the Treasury Multisig's on-chain activity are not confidential — transparency is core to how Aretia operates (see `PROTOCOL.md` §13–14).

## 10. No employment, no equity

This Agreement does not create an employment relationship, and does not grant Signer any equity, token allocation beyond what is stated in Section 5, or ownership interest in Aretia Finance LLC or Aretia Climate, LLC.
