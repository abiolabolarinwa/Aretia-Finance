Deploy the Aretia launch factory

This factory creates a token and opens its trading pool with the liquidity locked, in one transaction. Your own wallet signs and pays the gas for the deployment; the fee below is charged to people who launch tokens, not to you now. The fee, the fee address, the exchange and the minimum pool are fixed for ever once deployed, so check them before you approve.
