About

Climate finance has a trust gap. Pledges accelerate faster than verified action, and capital that moves often does so without proof it did what it claimed. Permissionless token trading on Solana generates enormous, continuous volume with no purpose beyond the trade itself. Aretia connects the two mechanically: a protocol-enforced fee turns a fixed share of that existing activity into standing climate capital, verified at every step against the public ledger, not a promise.

ACT is the native asset of the Aretia Climate Finance Protocol, an SPL Token-2022 asset that trades openly on Solana with no gatekeeping. A protocol-enforced share of every trade replenishes a climate catalyst fund that finances mitigation and adaptation projects, released against verified milestones, governed in layers rather than by one wallet.

Project update

Tokenomics parameters are locked and the mint mechanism is proven. Entity formation and the base treasury mechanism are legally reviewed — Aretia Climate, LLC and Aretia Finance LLC are both registered in Delaware. The expanded governance/project-financing architecture is new and still pending its own legal review. ACT has not launched and no live market data exists yet.

Our Mission

To ensure that climate capital moves on proof, not promises — enforced by code, verified in the open, and governed in layers so no single wallet ever decides where it goes.
