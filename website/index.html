About Aretia

Capital that speaks for itself.

A transparent protocol where the mechanism for generating climate capital is defined in advance, treasury movements are publicly observable, and project funding is released against measurable milestones — not a promise anyone has to trust.

Capital

Verify, Don't Trust

Four properties, each independently checkable.

01

Fixed Supply

1,000,000,000 ACT. Mint authority revoked immediately after the single initial mint.

02

Multisig Custody

2-of-3 treasury approval. No single wallet, including ours, can move capital alone.

03

Milestone Verified

Capital released against verified project progress — never a lump sum against a promise.

04

On-Chain Auditability

Every transaction is publicly observable on Solana, checkable by anyone.
