Our Mission

Climate finance built on proof, not promises.

Permissionless token trading generates enormous, continuous volume with no purpose beyond the trade itself. Aretia connects the two mechanically: a protocol-enforced fee turns a fixed share of that activity into standing climate capital, verified at every step against the public ledger.

Project Update

Tokenomics parameters are locked and the v2 mint mechanism is proven. Entity formation and the base treasury mechanism are legally reviewed: Aretia Climate, LLC and Aretia Finance LLC are both registered in Delaware. The expanded governance and project-financing architecture is new and still pending its own legal review. ACT has not launched and no live market data exists yet.

Staking & Access

The more ACT you stake, the earlier you see what's investable.

Projects that complete a full catalyst-fund cycle and clear underwriting are proposed to graduate into a separately instrumented investment opportunity, never routed through ACT itself. Staking tier decides who sees that opportunity first and in the most depth, not who's allowed to invest: eligibility to actually commit capital always depends on the holder's own jurisdiction and that instrument's own rules, never on ACT.

Step 1

Lock ACT for a chosen duration

Amount and duration together compute a Capital Access Score, verifiable on-chain by anyone.

Step 2

Build tier standing early

Staking now, before Stage Two exists, builds real duration credit a later staker simply won't have yet.

Step 3

See catalyzed projects first

Higher tiers get earlier notice and deeper access as projects clear underwriting, once this stage is built.

Common Questions

Answered plainly, traceable to the whitepaper or on-chain.

Has ACT launched?
No. The mint is live on Solana mainnet and the treasury multisig is live and tested, but no liquidity pool has been seeded yet, so ACT is not currently tradeable on any exchange. An initial, self-funded liquidity seed is planned. See the roadmap.
Does ACT show a name or logo in my wallet?
Yes, a real name and logo are attached on-chain via a Token-2022 MetadataPointer extension, and that metadata is permanently locked at the Metaplex-program level once set. See the full FAQ for exactly how.
Who controls the treasury?
No single party. The treasury is a Squads multisig requiring 2 of 3 independent signers to approve any outbound transaction, tested end to end with a real transaction on mainnet.
Has Aretia been independently audited?
Not yet. No formal, independent security audit has been completed, and that's stated plainly here rather than implied otherwise. The Verify tool is a free, instant first check, not a substitute for one.
