About

Climate finance has a trust gap. Pledges accelerate faster than verified action, and capital that moves often does so without proof it did what it claimed. Permissionless token trading on Solana generates enormous, continuous volume with no purpose beyond the trade itself — Aretia connects the two mechanically: a protocol-enforced fee turns a fixed share of that existing activity into standing climate capital, verified at every step against the public ledger, not a promise.

Our Mission

To ensure that climate capital moves on proof, not promises — enforced by code, verified in the open, and governed in layers so no single wallet ever decides where it goes.

Project insights

Why ACT

Permissionless token trading moves enormous volume on Solana with no purpose beyond the trade itself. Climate finance has the opposite problem: real funding need, and a widening trust gap between pledged and verified action — carbon credit retirements fell 7% in 2025 even as corporate climate commitments rose 227%. ACT redirects a slice of trading activity that already exists into a climate catalyst fund — standing capital, continuously replenished by trading activity, that finances climate mitigation and adaptation projects released against verified milestones, not a single promise from a single wallet.

What ACT isn't

Not a claim on any carbon credit. Not an investment contract, equity stake, or profit-sharing arrangement. Holding ACT creates no claim on treasury assets and no guaranteed value.

FAQ

What is ACT?

ACT (Aretia) is an SPL Token-2022 asset that trades openly on Solana, no gatekeeping. A fixed share of every trade is automatically withheld by the token contract and routed to a treasury that functions as a climate catalyst fund — standing capital, continuously replenished by trading activity, that finances mitigation and adaptation projects.

How does ACT fund climate projects?

Every transfer routes 4.1% through Solana's Token-2022 transfer-fee extension: 2% to a treasury multisig for project grants and on-chain carbon-credit retirements, 1% to liquidity, 1% burned, and 0.1% to fund the treasury's own monitoring and verification work.

Is ACT an investment?

No. Holding ACT creates no equity, profit share, or claim on treasury assets. It is a speculative, freely traded digital asset with no guaranteed value. See the whitepaper's full risk disclosure before relying on anything about ACT.

When does ACT launch?

No launch date is set. Legal review, devnet testing, project-partner vetting, and an independent program review all need to complete first — see the roadmap above for current status.

Where can I buy ACT?

The Buy ACT widget on this page is live and wired to the real mainnet mint, but no liquidity pool has been seeded yet — swaps will show "no route found" until it is. Nothing else needs to change here once the pool goes live; the widget will start returning routes automatically.

What is the 0.1% management fee for?

It funds the operating entity's cost of actually running the treasury responsibly — verifying registry certificates, reviewing partner impact reports, occasional site visits, and dashboard upkeep. It settles in its own dedicated wallet, separate from the treasury multisig, so it can never be merged into the project-funding total even by accident. Not yet funded — it only receives money once real trading volume generates fees to harvest.
