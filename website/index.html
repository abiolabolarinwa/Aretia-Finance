Our Mission

Catalytic capital that de-risks climate projects for commercial investment.

Climate mitigation and adaptation projects rarely fail for lack of capital in the system; they fail because the riskiest, earliest stage of a project's life can't clear the return threshold commercial and institutional investors require.

Aretia's treasury is built to close exactly that gap: a protocol-enforced fee turns ordinary, unrelated trading activity into standing catalytic capital, financing projects through that highest-risk stage so verified progress, not a pitch, becomes the basis on which commercial capital can eventually take over.
