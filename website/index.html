01 — About

Climate finance needs infrastructure it can trust.

Climate capital often moves through complex institutions, fragmented reporting systems, and long chains of intermediaries. Aretia proposes a different model.

A transparent protocol where the mechanism for generating climate capital is defined in advance, treasury movements are publicly observable, and project funding is released against measurable milestones.

03 — The Mechanism

From transfer to climate capital.

01

Transfer

ACT transactions generate a protocol-defined fee, withheld automatically by the Token-2022 standard itself — not by custom logic Aretia wrote.

02

Allocation

A fixed portion is directed toward the climate catalyst fund, with the remainder split between liquidity and protocol operations.

03

Treasury

Funds accumulate under multisig custody — a Squads vault requiring 2-of-3 independent signer approval to move.

04

Verification

Projects must demonstrate independently verified milestones before any tranche of funding is released.

05

Release

Capital is released in tranches against verified progress — never a lump sum against a single approval.

— Protocol Allocation

A fixed mechanism, not a discretionary promise.

3.5% Protocol fee, withheld on every transfer
2.0%Climate Catalyst Fund
1.0%Liquidity
0.5%Management

The allocation is defined by the protocol's on-chain configuration and is designed to make the relationship between activity and climate finance explicit. It is not permanently immutable — the mint's fee-rate authority sits with the treasury multisig, so a change requires the same 2-of-3 signer approval as any other treasury action, never a single party acting alone. 05 — Climate Finance

Capital moves where measurable climate outcomes can be demonstrated.

01

Renewable Energy

Verified OutputMetered generation capacity added.
DiligenceIndependent engineering certification and operating documentation.
02

Waste-to-Energy

Verified OutputWaste diverted and energy generated.
DiligenceFacility operating permits, throughput metering, emissions monitored against a stated baseline.
03

Carbon Reduction

Verified OutputEmissions avoided or removed.
DiligenceRegistry-issued credits (Verra, Gold Standard) or an equivalent third-party MRV protocol.
04

Technical Assistance

Verified OutputCapacity delivered — studies, training, MRV systems built.
DiligenceDeliverable-based milestones; independent review of the technical work product.
05

Flood Management & Reforestation

Verified OutputHectares restored; flood-risk reduction achieved.
DiligenceRemote-sensing canopy verification; hydrological modeling or post-event assessment.
