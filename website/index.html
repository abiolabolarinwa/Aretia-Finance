Common Questions

Answered plainly, traceable to the whitepaper or on-chain.

Has ACT launched?
No. The mint is live on Solana mainnet and the treasury multisig is live and tested, but no liquidity pool has been seeded yet, so ACT is not currently tradeable on any exchange. An initial, self-funded liquidity seed is planned. See the roadmap.
Why doesn't ACT show a name or logo in my wallet?
Mint authority was permanently revoked immediately after the single initial mint, which is what makes the fixed-supply claim verifiable rather than a promise. The cost is that no metadata can ever be attached afterward. See the full FAQ for the tradeoff.
Who controls the treasury?
No single party. The treasury is a Squads multisig requiring 2 of 3 independent signers to approve any outbound transaction, tested end to end with a real transaction on mainnet.
Has Aretia been independently audited?
Not yet. No formal, independent security audit has been completed, and that's stated plainly here rather than implied otherwise. The Verify tool is a free, instant first check, not a substitute for one.
