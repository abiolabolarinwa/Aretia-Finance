How it would work

01

Catalyst Fund grant

A project applies, gets scored, and receives grant capital -- no token, no repayment expected.

02

Verified operation

The project runs independently and reports verified performance data, the same way grant milestones are checked.

03

Institutional investment

Real capital comes from institutions and Aretia Finance Inc, a licensed PE-style vehicle -- not directly from the public.

04

Public project token

A separate, freely tradeable token launches against ACT for the community -- access and reputation, not a revenue claim.

Two instruments, two purposes

The design keeps real investment and public trading deliberately separate, so neither has to carry a job it isn't built for.

1

The investment round is private and off-chain. Institutions and Aretia Finance Inc fund the project on real terms -- this is where any real financial return lives, gated by proper licensing and counsel.

2

The project token is public and on-chain. Freely tradeable against ACT, carrying no revenue claim -- its value is access, reporting visibility, and community standing, not a promise of payout.

Marketplace questions.

Can I invest in a project through this page today?
No. This page is a concept preview. The only real, live mechanism on the site today is the Catalyst Fund grant intake at apply.html. Nothing here is an offer to sell a security or an investment.
What is a project token?
A freely tradeable, on-chain token for a specific project, paired against ACT. It doesn't represent ownership or a claim on the project's revenue -- it's meant to carry access and community reputation, kept separate from the actual investment instrument for legal and practical reasons.
When will this become real?
Not before the same five conditions already stated for Phase 5 on the roadmap are met: the operating entity verifiably registered, liquidity seeded and ACT trading, one full Catalyst Fund grant cycle completed end to end, an independent program review complete, and one investment vehicle structured properly with securities counsel.

The only real thing here today

Catalyst Fund grants are live now. Everything else on this page is the design record for what could come after.
