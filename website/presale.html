What happens when you buy

01

Contribute USDC, USDT, or SOL

Funds are escrowed on-chain, not swept anywhere, until the sale actually finalizes.

02

Window closes or hard cap hits

Soft cap met: escrow sweeps to the real treasury and vesting starts. Soft cap missed: everyone can refund.

03

25% unlocks immediately

The remaining 75% of your allocation vests linearly over the following 48 hours from TGE.

04

Claim as it vests

Claim any time; the program pays out whatever portion has vested since your last claim, net of the transfer fee.

How this differs from the transfer fee

Two separate mechanisms this project is careful never to conflate.

1

The presale raises initial capital to launch ACT and seed liquidity -- a one-time event with a fixed window and fixed terms.

2

The 3.5% transfer fee is the ongoing mechanism that funds the climate catalyst fund on every trade thereafter, indefinitely, independent of whether the presale happened at all.

Presale questions.

Can I buy ACT in the presale yet?
Not yet. The program is compiled and live on Solana devnet, and every instruction (buying, finalizing, claiming, refunding) has been tested there with real transactions, but it has not been deployed to mainnet. No real ACT can be purchased today.
What happens to my money if the soft cap isn't reached?
You get back exactly what you put in. Funds sit in on-chain escrow until the sale finalizes; if the $500,000 soft cap isn't met by the end of the window, the sale flips to a refunding state and each contributor calls a refund instruction themselves -- proven on devnet, including the case of a second refund attempt being correctly rejected.
Why does the vesting matter?
25% of your allocation unlocks immediately at the token generation event; the remaining 75% unlocks linearly over 48 hours. This has been tested across multiple claim checkpoints on devnet, confirming the cumulative claimed amount tracks correctly and nothing is either double-paid or left stranded once fully vested.
