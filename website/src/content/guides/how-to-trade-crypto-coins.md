Trading a coin means swapping one asset for another and hoping to be able to swap back at a better price. It is not saving, and it is not a way to earn a steady income. Most people who trade actively lose money after costs, so read this as a description of how it works, not a promise of what it will do for you. It is general education and not financial advice.

## Where you can trade

- **A centralised exchange** (a company's app): you deposit money, the company holds your assets, and you trade on its order book. Easier to start, and you depend on the company.
- **A decentralised exchange (DEX):** you keep your own assets in a wallet and swap against a pool of money on a blockchain. You can reach almost any token, including thousands that are worthless.
- **A wallet with swaps built in:** the same as a DEX, with the screens in one place. Aretia is of this kind, and also shows live token markets on six networks.

The [choosing guide](/learn/how-to-choose-a-crypto-trading-app) lists ten questions to ask of any of them.

## The steps, on a decentralised exchange

1. **Set up a wallet** and write the recovery phrase on paper. Never type it into a website or share it ([why](/learn/self-custody-wallet-vs-exchange)).
2. **Get the network's coin.** You need a little SOL for Solana, ETH for Ethereum, BNB for BNB Chain, and so on, to pay the network fee, even if you trade other tokens.
3. **Find the token and check it** before you swap: liquidity, age, who can change it, whether you can sell ([the seven checks](/learn/how-to-check-a-token-before-you-swap)).
4. **Swap a small amount first.** Look at the quote: price, fee, price impact and the minimum you will receive.
5. **Confirm in your wallet** only after you have read what you are signing.
6. **Swap back a small amount** to prove the exit works before you commit more.

## Costs people miss

- **The fee shown** and **the network fee**, which can be higher than the trade on a busy day.
- **Price impact:** a swap moves the price, more in a small pool ([the arithmetic](/learn/what-is-liquidity-in-a-token-pool)).
- **Slippage:** the price can change between your quote and your trade. Set a limit so a bad price cancels the trade instead of filling it.
- **Taxes:** in many countries each swap is a taxable event. Keep records.

## The mistakes that cost the most

- Trading money you cannot afford to lose.
- Buying because a price is rising and many people are talking about it.
- Using one huge position instead of small ones.
- Skipping the checks on a new token because it is "early".
- Trusting someone who contacts you first with a tip, a signal group, or a promise of returns. These are scams more often than not.
