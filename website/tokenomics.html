Here's how the mechanism works.

01

Transfer

Every ACT transfer withholds 3.5%, automatically, at the protocol level.

02

Treasury

Harvested fees settle into a 2-of-3 multisig, tested end to end on mainnet.

03

Verification

Projects clear category-specific diligence before reaching disbursement.

04

Disbursement

Capital releases in tranches, each one a public, permanent transaction.

05

Stake

Holders can lock ACT to build a Capital Access Score: the more staked and the longer it's locked, the earlier and deeper the priority access when catalyzed projects become investable. Tested on devnet, not live yet.
